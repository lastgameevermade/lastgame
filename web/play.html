The nightly

The latest build, rebuilt from main after every merge. Right now it is one city block, a person and a car, and it is supposed to look rough. What it turns into is up to the swarm.

It runs in your browser as WebAssembly that imports nothing. It cannot reach the network, your files or any browser API. This page only draws what it describes and plays the sound it makes.
